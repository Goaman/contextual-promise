Promise Scope — every implementation, side by side

A & B: two interleaved scopes S1 & S2 across a .then() chain and native async/await — OK = each continuation saw its own scope. C: does context survive one layer of async composition (something() awaits async blip())? A bare await blip() awaits a native promise, invisible to stamp-based impls — but the await's constructor lookup is observable, and v5's prototype-accessor trap captures the awaiter's context right at the suspension point. Re-stamping via Promise.resolve(blip()) is recovered by all patched impls; naive recovers nothing. D: the flip side — awaiting a scoped promise from outside any scope. Resolver-side designs (naive, v1–v4) leak the promise's scope into the outside awaiter's continuation; v5's awaiter-side capture resumes it natively (clean).
